Write to us: enricofanucchi@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Include the app version (at the bottom of Settings), your device and system, and the remote computer's VNC server (for example macOS Screen Sharing, TigerVNC, wayvnc, RealVNC). Never send passwords or private keys.

Getting started

  1. Turn on screen sharing on the remote computer: on a Mac in System Settings › General › Sharing › Screen Sharing; on Linux and Windows with a VNC server.
  2. In Tethera choose Add Server and enter the address and port (usually 5900). Nearby computers appear by themselves under Servers.
  3. Click the server: the session opens in its own window.

Frequently asked questions

How do I secure the connection?

In the server's settings turn on Use SSH Tunnel: screen and keyboard travel encrypted and the lock in the session bar turns green. The same SSH connection is used to transfer files.

How do I connect when I'm away from home?

With Tailscale or another VPN, use the computer's VPN address. Without a VPN, set Away from Home with an always-on SSH gateway on your network (for example a Raspberry Pi): no VNC ports need to be opened on the router.

How do I send files to the remote computer?

Drop files on the session window, or open Files in the session bar. The remote computer needs SSH turned on and the server needs the SSH tunnel.

The remote computer is a Mac: how do shortcuts work?

On Windows and Linux, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+A and Ctrl+Z become the ⌘ shortcuts on the Mac. Ctrl+V first pastes the text you copied on your computer.

How do I wake a sleeping computer?

Enter its MAC address in the server's settings (Wake-on-LAN section), then choose Wake from the server's menu. It works on the same local network.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete your servers in the app, then uninstall it. We keep no copies anywhere: see Privacy.
